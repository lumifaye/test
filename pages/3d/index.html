3D Worlds

These use perspective projection or 3D-style geometry, not just a flat particle effect with a new label.

  • Wireframe World · fly around a procedural wireframe landscape with WASD.
  • 3D Shape Lab · rotate a cube, pyramid, octahedron, or diamond.
  • Voxel Builder · poke around a tiny block world.
  • First-Person 3D Maze · raycast first-person maze.
  • 3D Globe · drag to rotate a procedural planet.
